Questions

Before you sign in.

Who sees my numbers?

Your team leader, who is the Admin. If you are in a QSM's downline, that QSM sees your answers too. Other members never see them.

What if I miss a question?

Each question stays open for three days after its date. After that it is marked Missed. Next month's question opens on its own, and you never have to answer several months at once.

Can I change my NeoLife ID?

No. It is your official number, so only your team leader can correct it, and every correction is logged. You can change your name, picture and other details yourself.

How does a team leader create an account?

Choose "Register as a team leader". Enter your name, team name, email, NeoLife ID and a password, then open the confirmation link we email you. You get your TeamPulse ID and an empty team. Add your members from the Sign-ups tab. Each member gets their own ID and a link to set their password.

How do I get an account?

Your team leader adds you. You do not register yourself. You then get a welcome email with your TeamPulse ID and a link to set your own password. The link works for 7 days.

I forgot my password.

Choose "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in screen and enter your TeamPulse ID. We email a reset link to the address your team leader has on file. Your team leader can also reset it for you.

Do I need to install an app?

No. TeamPulse runs in your phone's browser. You sign in with your TeamPulse ID and your password.

Is this official NeoLife software?

No. TeamPulse is an independent tool for one NeoLife team. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by NeoLife International.

Why does my team use the 15th as a start date?

Your team treats the 15th as day one of the next month to keep a sense of urgency toward each month's target. TeamPulse shows that countdown, but all numbers and ranks follow the normal calendar month, like NeoLife does.
